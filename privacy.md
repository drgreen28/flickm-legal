# Flickm Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026

Flickm ("the game") is a puzzle game published by Green Hollow Apps LLC ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what information the game handles and what choices you have. It applies to the game on
Android and iOS.

## The short version

- Your progress, scores, power-ups, skins and settings stay **on your device**. There are no
  accounts and no game servers, and we do not receive any of it.
- The game has **no ads**, **no analytics** and **no tracking**.
- Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. We never see your
  payment details.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores the following on your device only:

- your level progress, stars and best scores
- your power-up counts, the skins you own and the skin you have equipped
- your settings (sound, music, vibration, aim guide, color symbols, reduced motion)
- whether you have seen the tutorial
- a record of completed purchases (the product and the store's transaction ID), so that each
  purchase is granted once and skins can be restored

We do not receive any of this. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. **Options → Reset
progress** clears your levels, stars, scores and tutorial flag, and keeps your purchases and
settings.

If your device backs up app data (for example Android backup or iCloud), this information may be
included in that backup. Those backups are controlled by your device settings and handled by
Google or Apple, not by us.

## Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases of power-ups, continues and cosmetic skins. They are
processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your name, email
address or payment details. The store tells the game which product was bought and gives it a
transaction ID, which the game keeps on your device. When you choose **Restore purchases**, the
game asks the store which skins you own.

Learn how the stores handle your information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Game engine

Flickm is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited technical information, such as
device model, operating system version, and crash or performance diagnostics, to maintain and
improve its engine. This information is not used to identify you, and we do not receive it.
See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## Links

**Options → Privacy policy** and **Options → Terms of service** open these pages in your web
browser. Your browser's own privacy terms apply there.

## Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling
the game removes everything it stores on your device. For information held by Google, Apple or
Unity, see the links above.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws),
you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to certain
processing. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we have none to provide, correct
or delete. If you have any question about this policy, contact us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, and the effective date
above shows when it last changed.
